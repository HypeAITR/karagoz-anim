# Sitting down

Standing up in reverse: the standing pose and the sitting pose are the keys. The knees bend and the hips go back and
down while the torso leans forward to balance; the hips come down onto the seat **last**; then the torso settles back
and a small settle follows. The head and hips only go down; they do not rise again before the seat. Do not make a held
key out of the half-seated moment.

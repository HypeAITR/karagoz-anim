# Key poses

- **One key pose per idea**: listening, reacting, reaching, standing. Usually 3–4 story poses per line at most
  (`lint.pose_per_line_max`). A listener gets one held pose and blinks.
- **A pose must read as a silhouette**: the hand that matters is outside the body outline, the head turned where
  the character looks, the weight visible (which leg, which seat).
- **Each pose is drawn from the previous one.** The image model keeps what the text does not change and redraws
  what it does. So the text must name what stays ("right foot still raised at knee height, left foot still flat on
  the floor") as concretely as what changes. "Same legs" is not enough; it was redrawn three different ways in one
  shot when only that was written.
- **A pose is a held position, not a moment inside a movement.** Do not make a key pose out of the middle of a
  motion (half risen, mid-step). Held, it freezes the motion halfway — see the sections on body moves.

# What looks wrong — the checklist

Checkers always get this section in full.

- **A jump:** two drawings in a row where the head, hips or a hand change place much more than their neighbours do.
- **Going the wrong way:** down while standing up, back while reaching.
- **A frozen half-move:** a mid-motion drawing held for many frames.
- **Props:** two copies of a prop, a prop in a hand that should be empty, a prop teleporting, a prop's shape wrong.
- **Off the path:** an inbetween that is not between its neighbours (back to the old pose, or aside).
- **Identity:** three arms, a missing hand, a changed face, clothes or colours.
- **Facing:** a mirrored drawing (facing the wrong way; text on clothes reversed).
- **Extras:** furniture, a floor shadow, background or text drawn into a character drawing.
- **Scale and contact:** a character too big or too small for the room, feet not reaching the floor while sitting.

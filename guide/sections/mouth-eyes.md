# Mouths and eyes

They come from a library made once per pose and are placed by the engine on the face of the held drawing. They are
timed from the voice (a mouth shape starts slightly before its sound, closes on m/b/p). Pose descriptions never
describe the mouth beyond "mouth closed". The Timer may add a mouth `edit` only to make a key vowel wider or a word
land, with a note why.
